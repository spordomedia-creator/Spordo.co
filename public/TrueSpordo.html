About Spordo

THE FIELD IS OURS

"We didn't build this for investors. We built it because we kept showing up and finding locked gates."

Pier 40 — Hudson River Park, West Village
The origin

WHERE THIS
STARTED

It started with a locked gate at Pier 40 on a Saturday morning. We'd organized a crew — three trains across two boroughs — and found a permit notice taped to the fence. No warning. No digital update. Just a paper sign and an afternoon gone.

That same week, four different groups said the same thing happened to them. At Astoria Park. In Fort Greene. At the fields behind Flushing Meadows.

New York City has one of the most contested collections of public athletic space on earth. Five boroughs, eight million people, and a permit system that was built for the administrator — not the athlete.

Spordo was built to close that gap. Real-time permit data, surfaced for the person with a ball in their hand.

Rucker Park — Harlem
No gatekeepers

WHO WE
BUILT THIS FOR

The soccer player in Hunts Point who scouts fields on Sunday morning. The cricket team in Richmond Hill navigating NYRPA permits without any tools. The youth basketball league in Flatbush organizing six courts and three divisions by text message.

This isn't a startup. No venture capital. No growth hacking. Spordo is a free public tool built by people who play in these parks.

We surface NYC Parks permit data so any player, any coach, any kid with a ball can know before they go. No paywall. No login. No nonsense.

Questions

THE HONEST ANSWERS

Is Spordo actually free to use?

Yes, completely. No login, no subscription, no paywalled data. Every field, every permit status, every map view — free to anyone with a phone. That's not a marketing line. It's how we built it, and it's how we plan to keep it.

Where does my money actually go?

Server costs, map tile hosting, NYC Parks data access, and development time. We don't pay ourselves salaries from this. Spordo is a community project, and donations go toward keeping the infrastructure running and the data current.

Who built Spordo?

A small team of New Yorkers who grew up playing in these parks. No venture funding, no corporate backing. We built this because we needed it — and because nobody else was building it for us.

Can I cancel a monthly contribution?

Yes, anytime. Monthly contributions are processed through Stripe. You'll get a receipt when you donate and can cancel from the Stripe customer portal with one click. No questions asked.

Article 8 — Precise Geolocation Practices

This section gives detailed rules for SPORDO's use of GPS-level location data and emphasizes that location is processed ephemerally rather than stored as a long-term archive.

When enabled by the user, SPORDO may collect precise geolocation data, including GPS coordinates supplied by the device or browser, solely to power the feature that shows nearby soccer fields, basketball courts, and tennis courts on an interactive map. SPORDO's use of location data is limited to delivering this user-requested feature and related troubleshooting, safety, fraud-prevention, and legal compliance needs.

SPORDO processes precise GPS coordinates ephemerally and transiently in memory solely as needed to calculate and display the proximity of sports courts and related facilities within the user's browser or device session. SPORDO does not permanently store, log, or maintain historical archives of users' precise physical coordinates on its backend servers and does not build long-term movement profiles based on precise GPS traces.

Users may decline location access, disable browser or device location permissions, or stop using location-enabled portions of the Service at any time. Disabling location access may reduce or prevent nearby-court discovery features from functioning as intended.
